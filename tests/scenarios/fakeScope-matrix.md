## Proposed `tool-sweep` scenario (agent, all three fakes)

It calls the tools that only Group B scenarios call: cw_ask_user, cw_report_merge, cw_update_tasks, cw_read_section, cw_doc_outline, cw_put_back, cw_request_builder, cw_ask_assistant, cw_notify. Its one check per tool: the server's MCP log (CW_TEST_MCP_LOG) has the call, and its reply is Combwork's own (worked, or refused with a rule).
