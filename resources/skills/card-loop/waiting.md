# Card loop: waits and other cards

**A loop wake to come:** while you wait on the other side's move, loop wakes bring it with no watch: as builder, the verdict on your card in Review (no request of the reviewer's left to answer) or your design's or follow-up's answer; as reviewer, the next submission. Only while the project's loop wakes are on (its workflow option **Wake reviewers and builders**) and you have your Combwork tools: then a watch a notice or its limit ended needs no restarting, and its limit is no question for the user. Otherwise, or for what a loop wake doesn't bring (a comment you asked for, another card, a dependency), start the watch (SKILL.md, Waiting). A design's or follow-up's own `until` still holds.

**A watch reply saying neither** ("Waiting for…", "Already: …"): watch again once; still neither, `cw_notify` the user.

**No wake possible** (the tool says so): wait in the call, `timeoutSeconds` up to 840, passing its `since` on.

**Another agent's card** that yours depends on: its event tells you that card changed, not yours; Passed means reviewed, not merged. **A card that left your watch** (reassigned, reviewed by another): drop it and carry on, without asking. **A blocked card** is paused, not gone: it stays on your list, and the next card waits for it.
