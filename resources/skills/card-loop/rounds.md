# Card loop: rounds and when to ask

A round is one submission and its review; a card that passes its first review passed in round 1.
- **The round limit** is the lane's rounds. Where the project's rules are enforced, Hive counts failed reviews and, at the limit, asks the user itself (carry on, split, accept with follow-ups, take over) and pauses the lane: submitting is refused until their answer comes to your inbox. With the rules off nothing counts them, and a card that keeps failing is your judgement, as below.
- **A finding that comes back** (the fix missed part of it): the reviewer says so in the failed verdict ("recurring from round 2") and the loop carries on. When the same kind keeps coming back, a design step (builder.md) or a simpler option for the user beats another round. A finding the builder **disputes**, rather than missed, is a design question: stop and ask.
- **Stop and ask** on a design question the card doesn't answer, on a blocked card, on a merge step that needs the user, when the wait passes with no change on what no loop wake brings, or when a card of the loop is archived or gone. Builder or reviewer, use `hive_ask_user` on the card where it is (`kind: "decide"`; `"approve"` for an OK only they can give, such as a push): it shows under For you and wakes whoever watches the lane. A card archived or gone can't take one: `hive_notify` the user, and leave it as it is. A comment alone reaches no one: what only the Assistant may do, once decided (parking a card, say), goes to it: `hive_ask_assistant`. No further verdict or round meanwhile; their answer comes to your inbox: carry on from where you asked.
